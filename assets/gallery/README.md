# Our Work — photos & videos

Drop photos OR short videos in THIS folder and they automatically appear in the
"Our Work" gallery on the website — no code changes needed.

## How to add (from your phone or computer)
1. Go to: https://github.com/gpracer68/the-riders-garage/upload/master/assets/gallery
   (must be signed in as gpracer68)
2. Drag in your files
3. Click **Commit changes**
4. Wait ~1 minute — they show up on the site.

## Photos
- JPG or PNG. Keep each under ~3 MB so the page loads fast.

## Videos
- Use **MP4** (H.264) — plays on every phone and browser.
- iPhone .MOV clips often DON'T play in browsers; set Camera to "Most
  Compatible" (Settings > Camera > Formats) or export as MP4 first.
- **Keep clips short and under ~40 MB** — GitHub blocks web uploads over 100 MB
  and warns over 25 MB. A 10-30 second highlight is perfect.
- Videos show a red ▶ play button in the grid; tap to play full-size.

## Captions & order
- The caption under each item comes from the file name:
  `2023-ktm-topend-rebuild.jpg` shows as "2023 ktm topend rebuild".
- Control order by starting names with numbers: `01-...`, `02-...`
